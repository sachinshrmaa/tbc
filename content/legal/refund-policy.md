**All cohort fees are non-refundable.**

Each cohort has a small, fixed number of seats, and your seat is held for you from the moment your payment is confirmed. Once you've paid, I can't offer a refund, whether or not you attend the sessions.

Please read the cohort page carefully before paying: the schedule, prerequisites and syllabus are all listed there. If you have any doubts, email me **before** you pay and I'll help you decide whether the cohort is right for you.

## Missed sessions

Every live session is recorded and shared with the cohort, so you can catch up on anything you miss. Missing sessions doesn't make you eligible for a refund.

## If TBC cancels a cohort

If I cancel a cohort before it starts, you'll get a **full refund** to the UPI account you paid from, within 7 working days.

## Duplicate or incorrect payments

If you paid twice by mistake, or paid more than the listed fee, email me your transaction IDs and I'll return the extra amount.
