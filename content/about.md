## Building Wicklog

Alongside teaching, I'm the founder of **Wicklog**, which I build part-time. Wicklog is where I practise everything I teach: picking a problem worth solving, cutting it down to something small enough to ship, building it quickly with AI, and then doing the hard part — getting real people to use it and pay for it.

That's why TBC cohorts end with a launched product, not a certificate. I want you to leave with what I wish I'd had when I started: the confidence to take your own idea from a blank screen to a live product, and the habits to keep improving it.

## Before Wicklog

I worked as a software engineer, building frontend products at a cloud-based startup. I studied Computer Engineering — a Diploma with distinction, then a Bachelor's at SIST.

## Why I teach

There are millions of things to learn for any skill, and nobody has figured out all of them. Everybody knows a thing or two, so I pass on what I know, and help you get good at figuring out the rest yourself.

You can find my open-source work on [GitHub](https://github.com/sachinshrmaa).
