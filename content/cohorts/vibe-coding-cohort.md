Most people use AI like a search box and get random results. This cohort teaches you to use it like a **senior engineer on your team** - one you can direct, review, and trust.

In 12 live sessions you go from idea to launched product. You will learn how AI actually works (so you know *why* some prompts work and others fail), then build a complete full-stack app step by step: UI, database, authentication, integrations, security, and deployment. Every day is hands-on - you build your own project alongside the live walkthrough, not a throwaway demo.

## What you will walk away with

- A **live product** on the internet that real people can use
- A deep understanding of how LLMs work, so you get the most out of any AI tool
- A repeatable workflow - plan, prompt, build, debug, ship - that you can use on every future project
- Your own **rules file and prompt library**
- The confidence to launch, get feedback, and keep iterating

## How it works

- **12 live sessions**, 1-2 hours each (typically about 90 minutes), Monday to Friday over 2 weeks
- Each session: short recap, concept demo, live build-along, and a small assignment
- **Doubt support** in a private community group between sessions
- Session recordings shared for anyone who misses a class
- Capstone **Demo Day** on the final session

## What you need to know up front

This is a focused build-and-ship program, not a computer science degree. AI tools write most of the code - your job is to learn to direct, verify, and launch. By the end you will understand enough of what is happening under the hood to debug problems and make sound decisions. We use free tiers of GitHub, Vercel, and Supabase, so your only possible extra cost is an AI tool subscription (a free-friendly path is covered in the setup guide).

**Seats are limited to 25** so every student gets real feedback on their project.
